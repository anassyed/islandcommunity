## 📊 Methodology

### Survey Design

The assessment used **"The Island: Building Our Community"** - a scenario-based approach where members faced 10 survival scenarios, choosing their MOST and LEAST appealing tasks from 4 options each.

**Scoring System**:

- MOST appealing choice: **+2 points**
- LEAST appealing choice: **-1 point**
- Score range per category: **-10 to +20**
